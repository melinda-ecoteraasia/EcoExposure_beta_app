2. Take PRE photos

Use water before adding practice powder. Take one top-down photo on each background. Pop open the photo box if lighting is uneven. Too dark? Fold its top flaps outward.

3. Optional practice powder

To rehearse the handling step, use the supplied Demo Reagent or a little turmeric powder as directed for practice. Keep the MP/NP test reagent for real samples.

Practice only. This exercise does not detect microplastics or nanoplastics. You can also continue using water alone.

For a real test, follow the kit instruction to avoid stirring or shaking after adding reagent.

No reaction timer in Practice. Tap Next when you are ready to rehearse POST photos.

No login, kit verification, database saves or reaction timer is involved. Photos stay in memory until you restart or leave.
